2026 DEZHU Cup Tennis Open

The 2026 DEZHU Cup Tennis Open is one of the premier junior tennis events in northern Atlanta. This year’s tournament featured singles competitions across four age divisions, showcasing young athletes’ talent and competitive spirit. All matches will be played on the hard courts of Alpha Tennis Academy (2650 Olde Towne Pkwy, Duluth), providing a high-quality playing environment. We sincerely thank our generous sponsors for their invaluable support. Their contributions made this event possible and helped create a meaningful experience for all participants.


Match Format

Match Format for Boys' 10U and Girls' 10U

Matches will be played as best of 3 short sets, with a 10-point match tiebreak in lieu of the third set. Each set is played as a short set: A player must win 4 games with at least a 2-game margin to win the set; If the score reaches 4–4, a 7-point tiebreak will be played to decide the set; All games are played using no-ad scoring (the first player to win 4 points wins the game; at deuce, the next point decides the game).

Match Format for 12 & Up

Matches will be played as best of 3 sets, with a 10-point match tiebreak in lieu of the third set.

Default NO-SHOW Policy

Players are expected to play all matches including main draw and consolation. Any player who does not report to the court at the scheduled time will be defaulted (recorded as a loss).

Refund Policy

After April 10th, no changes will be made to the draw and no refunds will be issued.
